Orders & Products

What exactly am I buying?

Customs4Daez sells digital overlay and controller skin designs — image/video files built for OBS and Streamlabs, or presets for our online customizer. These are digital products; we do not ship physical controllers or hardware.

How do I receive my purchase?

Digital files are delivered electronically after checkout — typically via a download link or email. There's nothing shipped in the mail.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders are delivered within minutes to a few hours. If you haven't received anything after 24 hours, please contact us.

Are your controllers/designs licensed by a console manufacturer?

No. Every Customs4Daez design is our own original work, and Customs4Daez is an independent creator brand, not affiliated with or endorsed by any console or platform manufacturer.

Customizer & Uploads

Can I upload my own logo or image?

Yes, our customizer lets you add your own logo or artwork. You're responsible for making sure you own the rights to anything you upload — see our Terms of Service for details.

What software do I need to use an overlay?

Our overlays are built for OBS Studio and Streamlabs. Add the downloaded file as a browser/video source, and you're set — no plugins required.

Payments & Refunds

What payment methods do you accept?

We process payments securely through Stripe. We never see or store your full card details.

Can I get a refund?

Because our products are digital and delivered instantly, most sales are final once files are delivered. Exceptions apply for non-delivery, duplicate charges, or a defective file. Full details are in our Refund Policy.

Can I resell or redistribute a design I bought?

No. Purchases grant you a personal-use license for your own stream/channel. Reselling, redistributing, or sublicensing a design is not permitted — see our Terms of Service.

Community

How do I get my clip featured?

Send a clip (1080p or higher, 60 seconds or less, with a C4D overlay visible) to Customs4daez@gmail.com. See the Community page for full guidelines.
