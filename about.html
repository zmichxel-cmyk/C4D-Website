About Us

The story behind Customs4Daez.

Who We Are

Customs4Daez was built for streamers and content creators who want their setup to look as sharp as their gameplay. What started as a small side project designing controller overlays has grown into a full catalog of pro-grade overlays, custom controller skins, and an in-browser customizer — all built to drop straight into OBS or Streamlabs with zero hassle.

What We Do

We design digital overlays and controller skins optimized for streaming: clean, low-CPU-impact assets that layer directly into your broadcast software, plus a showroom of pre-made designs and a customizer for building something that's entirely yours.

Our Community

Customs4Daez is more than a storefront — it's a community of streamers who share clips, swap feedback, and help shape what we build next. Check out the Community page to get involved, submit a clip, or leave a suggestion.

Original Designs

Every design sold through Customs4Daez is our own original work. Customs4Daez is an independent creator brand, not affiliated with or endorsed by any console or platform manufacturer.
